## Project Overview
This qualitative case study investigates the individual, technological, and organizational barriers affecting the adoption of Electronic Medical Record (EMR) systems in a selected hospital in Ethiopia, and identifies practical strategies to overcome them. While manual paper-based systems remain common, they create bottlenecks in retrieving patient histories, coordinating care across departments, and managing growing record volumes. Through semi-structured interviews and institutional document review with healthcare professionals, medical records officers, IT personnel, and administrators, this study seeks to propose actionable recommendations for improving digital record management and hospital operational efficiency.
