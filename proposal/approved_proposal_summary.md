# Approved Research Proposal Summary
**Title:** Digitizing Paper Records in Hospitals: Overcoming Adoption Barriers to Improve Patient Care and Efficiency by identifying Factors Affecting the Adoption of Electronic Medical Record Systems in Hospitals in Ethiopia  
**Submission Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Advisor:** Mr. Ermias Abebe  

## Research Questions
1. What individual, technological, and organizational barriers affect the adoption of electronic medical record systems in the selected hospital?
2. What strategies can the hospital implement to overcome these barriers and improve patient information management and operational efficiency?

## Research Objectives
* **General Objective:** To investigate the barriers affecting the adoption of electronic medical record systems in the selected hospital and identify strategies for improving patient information management and operational efficiency.
* **Specific Objectives:**
  1. To identify and examine the individual, technological, and organizational barriers affecting electronic medical record adoption in the selected hospital.
  2. To identify practical strategies for overcoming the identified barriers and improving patient information management and operational efficiency.
