# Semi-Structured Interview Guide (v1.0 — Pre-Pilot)
**Study:** Factors Affecting the Adoption of Electronic Medical Record (EMR) Systems in Hospitals in Ethiopia  
**Target Participants:** Healthcare professionals, medical records officers, IT personnel, and hospital administrators.

## Part 1: Informed Consent & Confidentiality Script
* Introduce the study purpose, voluntary participation, right to withdraw at any time, and request permission to record/take notes.
* Confirm that no patient-identifying data will be collected and participant identities will be anonymized.

## Part 2: Participant Background & Current Workflow
1. What is your current role in the hospital, and how are you involved in managing or accessing patient records?
2. Can you walk me through how patient records are currently created, stored, and shared across departments in your daily work?

## Part 3: Individual, Technological, and Organizational Barriers
3. **Individual Factors:** How comfortable are staff members with using digital tools and EMR interfaces? What gaps in digital literacy or computer skills have you observed?
4. **Technological Factors:** What challenges exist regarding hardware availability, network connectivity, power reliability, or EMR software usability?
5. **Organizational Factors:** How would you describe the level of staff training, ongoing IT technical support, and management commitment toward EMR adoption?
6. **Lived Experience Prompt:** Can you describe a specific instance where the current record system (paper or electronic) either helped or stood in the way of timely patient care?

## Part 4: Strategies & Recommendations
7. What practical measures, training programs, or infrastructure improvements should hospital management prioritize to ensure successful EMR adoption?
